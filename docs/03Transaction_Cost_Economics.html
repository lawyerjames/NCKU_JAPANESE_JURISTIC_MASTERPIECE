Transaction Cost Economics

Handbook of New Institutional Economics, pp. 41–65 （新制度經濟學手冊，第41-65頁）

作者： Oliver E. Williamson (Nobel Laureate in Economic Sciences)

3. Transaction Cost Economics

3. 交易成本經濟學
Transaction cost economics/trænˈzæk.ʃən kɒst ˌiː.kəˈnɒm.ɪks/ is an effort to better understand complex economic organization by selectively joining law, economics, and organization theory. As against neoclassical economics, which is predominantly concerned with price and output, relies extensively on marginal analysis, and describes the firm as a production function (which is a technological construction), transaction cost economics (TCE) is concerned with the allocation of economic activity across alternative modes of organization (markets, firms, bureaus, etc.), employs discrete structural analysis, and describes the firm as a governance structure/ˈɡʌv.ən.əns ˈstrʌk.tʃər/ (which is an organizational construction). Real differences notwithstanding, orthodoxy and TCE are in many ways complements — one being more well-suited to aggregation in the context of simple market exchange, the other being more well-suited to the microanalytics of complex contracting and nonmarket organization.
譯文
交易成本經濟學（Transaction Cost Economics）旨在透過選擇性地結合法律學、經濟學與組織理論，以更深入瞭解複雜的經濟組織。相較於主要關注價格與產出、高度依賴邊際分析，並將企業描述為生產函數（一種技術構建）的新古典經濟學，交易成本經濟學（TCE）關注的是經濟活動在不同組織模式（市場、企業、官僚機構等）之間的配置，採用離散結構分析，並將企業描述為一種治理結構（一種組織構建）。儘管存在實質差異，正統經濟學與TCE在許多方面互為補充——前者更適用於簡單市場交易背景下的總量分析，後者則更適用於複雜契約與非市場組織的微觀分析。

1. THE LENSES OF CHOICE AND CONTRACT

1. 選擇的視角與契約的視角
Big Ideas
譯文
大思想 (Big Ideas)
Hal Varian has recently distinguished between important ideas and Big Ideas and describes Ronald Coase’s classic paper, “The Nature of the Firm” (1937) as a Big Idea (2002, p. C2). Although there is widespread agreement on this, the nature of the big idea took a long time to register. Thus as of 1972, thirty-five years after the publication of “The Nature of the Firm,” Coase described his 1937 article as “much cited and little used” (1972, p. 63). It was much cited because it was onto something important, perhaps even big. But it was little used because the big idea was only dimly perceived and/or lacked operationalization/ˌɒp.ər.æʃ.ən.əl.aɪˈzeɪ.ʃən/.
譯文
Hal Varian 最近區分了「重要思想」與「大思想」（Big Ideas），並將羅納德·高斯（Ronald Coase）的經典論文《企業的性質》（1937年）描述為一個大思想（2002，第C2頁）。儘管對此有廣泛共識，但這個大思想的本質花了很長時間才被世人深刻理解。因此，直到1972年——在《企業的性質》發表35年後——高斯仍將他1937年的文章描述為「被頻繁引用卻鮮少被真正使用」（1972，第63頁）。它被頻繁引用是因為它觸及了重要、甚至是大思想的議題；但它鮮少被真正使用，是因為這個大思想當時僅被模糊地感知，且缺乏具體的操作化（operationalization）。
The Sciences of Choice and Contract
譯文
選擇的科學與契約的科學
The essence of the Coasian contribution has been variously described. For the purposes of TCE, I contend that the overarching big idea was to move from choice to contract: bring the lens of contract systematically to bear on economic phenomena of all kinds. For many transactions, of which the make-or-buy decision is one (Coase, 1937), the contractual structure is easily recognized. Other transactions, such as the externality problem (Coase, 1960), needed to be reformulated to bring out their latent contractual features. The object, in these and other cases described herein, is to uncover previously neglected but, often, consequential features, the discovery of which often leads to different and, sometimes, deeper understandings than the orthodox lens of choice affords.
譯文
高斯貢獻的精髓曾被各式各樣地描述。就交易成本經濟學而言，我主張其核心大思想是從「選擇」轉向「契約」：系統性地將契約視角應用於各類經濟現象。對於許多交易（例如高斯1937年研究的「自行生產還是外購」決策），契約結構很容易被識別。而其他交易，例如外部性問題（高斯1960年），則需要重新表述以凸顯其潛在的契約特徵。其目的在於揭示過往被忽視但通常影響深遠的特徵，這些發現往往帶來比正統「選擇視角」更深刻且不同的理解。

2. THE PARADIGM PROBLEM: VERTICAL INTEGRATION

2. 範例問題：垂直整合 (Vertical Integration)
Contract is an encompassing concept. Rather than treat the issue in its full generality, it will be instructive to begin with a specific puzzle of economic organization, ideally one for which other contractual issues will turn out to be variations on a theme. The obvious transaction with which to begin is that of vertical integration/ˈvɜː.tɪ.kəl ˌɪn.tɪˈɡreɪ.ʃən/, or, in more mundane terms, the make-or-buy decision. Not only is the make-or-buy decision the transaction on which Coase focused in 1937, but it has a prior and continuing history of importance within economics. Coase’s classic article opens with a basic puzzle: Why does a firm emerge at all in a specialized exchange economy? If the answer resides in entrepreneurship, why is coordination “the work of the price mechanism in one case and the entrepreneur in the other”?
譯文
契約是一個包羅萬象的概念。與其包山包海地處理此議題，不如從經濟組織的一個具體謎題開始，理想情況下，其他契約議題最終都會成為該主題的延伸變奏。最顯而易見的起步交易是垂直整合（vertical integration），或者用更俗俗的話來說，即「自行生產還是外購」（make-or-buy）的決策。這不僅是高斯1937年聚焦的交易，在經濟學中亦具歷史與持續的重要性。高斯的經典論文以一個基本謎題開篇：為什麼在專業化交換經濟中會出現企業？如果答案在於企業家精神，那麼為什麼協調在某些情況下是「價格機制的作用」，而在另一些情況下卻是「企業家的作用」？

3. THE RUDIMENTS

3. 基本要素 (The Rudiments)
(a) Human Actors: Bounded Rationality & Opportunism
譯文
(a) 人類行為者：有限理性與機會主義
If “Nothing is more fundamental in setting our research agenda and informing our research methods than our view of the nature of the human beings whose behavior we are studying” (Simon, 1985, p. 303), then economists and other social scientists are well-advised to describe the key cognitive attributes of human actors. Simon’s view of cognition is that the usual hyperrationality assumption be supplanted by bounded rationality/ˈbaʊn.dɪd ˌræʃ.əˈnæl.ə.ti/ — behavior that is “intendedly rational, but only limitedly so” (1957a, p. xxiv). TCE concurs that bounded rationality is the appropriate cognitive assumption and takes the chief lesson of bounded rationality for the study of contract to be that all complex contracts are unavoidably incomplete/ˌɪn.kəmˈpliːt/. TCE also pushes beyond frailty of motive to make provision for opportunism/ˌɒp.əˈtjuː.nɪ.zəm/. Opportunism has reference to exceptions — outliers where there is a lot at stake and parties are often observed to defect from the spirit of cooperation to insist on the letter of the incomplete contract.
譯文
如果如赫伯特·西蒙（Herbert Simon）所言：「在設定研究議程與確立研究方法時，沒有什麼比我們對研究對象（人類）本質的看法更為根本」，那麼經濟學家與社會科學家應詳加描述人類行為者的關鍵認知特徵。西蒙認為，常規的「超理性」假設應被「有限理性」（bounded rationality）所取代——即「意圖追求理性，但能力有限」的行為。交易成本經濟學（TCE）認同有限理性是適當的認知假設，並認為有限理性對契約研究的核心啟示為：所有複雜契約皆無可避免地具有不完備性（incomplete）。此外，TCE更進一步防範「機會主義」（opportunism）——即當利益攸關時，當事人在利益誘惑下背離合作精神、硬性堅持不完備契約字面條款的自利行為。
(b) Unit of Analysis: Asset Specificity
譯文
(b) 分析單位：資產專屬性 (Asset Specificity)
The natural unit of analysis for lens of contract purposes is the transaction. Transaction cost economics holds that three dimensions that have pervasive ramifications for governance are: asset specificity/ˈæset ˌspes.ɪˈfɪs.ə.ti/ (which takes a variety of forms — physical, human, site, dedicated, brand name), the disturbances to which transactions are subject, and the frequency with which transactions recur. The absence of asset specificity describes the ideal transaction in law and economics for which competition works well: “sharp in by clear agreement; sharp out by clear performance”. As asset specificity builds up, however, bilateral dependency develops, and in combination with uncertainty, contractual complications set in.
譯文
就契約視角而言，最自然的分析單位是「交易」（Transaction）。交易成本經濟學認為，對治理結構產生廣泛影響的三個關鍵維度是：資產專屬性（asset specificity，包括實體資產、人力資產、地理位置、專用資產與品牌名稱等多種型態）、交易面臨的干擾/不確定性，以及交易發生的頻率。缺乏資產專屬性的交易代表了法經濟學中的理想交易，市場競爭在此運作良好；然而隨著資產專屬性的提升，雙邊依賴關係逐漸形成，加上不確定性的影響，契約複雜性便油然而生。

6. CONCLUSIONS & PUBLIC POLICY

6. 結論與公共政策
Robert Solow’s prescription for doing good economics is set out in three injunctions: keep it simple; get it right; make it plausible (2001, p. 111). To this, moreover, I would add a fourth injunction: derive refutable implications to which the relevant microanalytic data are brought to bear. TCE responds to the injunction of keeping it simple by taking economizing on transaction costs to be the main case. The logic of economic organization is that of discriminating alignment/dɪˈskrɪm.ɪ.neɪ.tɪŋ əˈlaɪn.mənt/: aligning transactions, which differ in their attributes, with governance structures, which differ in their cost and competence. There is broad agreement that work of a transaction cost economics kind has helped to transform our understanding of complex contracting and economic organization, and that applications outside of economics are growing and will continue.
譯文
羅伯特·索洛（Robert Solow）對做好經濟學研究提出了三條誡律：保持簡單、確保正確、使其具說服力。對此，我會再加上第四條誡律：推導出可證偽的推論，並將微觀分析數據應用於實證檢驗。交易成本經濟學透過將「節約交易成本」作為核心主線來回應「保持簡單」的誡律。經濟組織的邏輯在於「鑑別性匹配」（discriminating alignment）：將具備不同屬性的交易，與具備不同成本與能力的治理結構進行最適匹配。世人廣泛公認，交易成本經濟學的工作徹底改變了我們對複雜契約與經濟組織的理解，且其在經濟學之外的應用正在並將繼續蓬勃發展。
